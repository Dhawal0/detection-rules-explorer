AWS IAM Role Created with External Trust

Last updated 2 days ago on 2026-10-23

About

Detects a successful AWS IAM CreateRole event whose trust policy allows sts:AssumeRole by a principal in another AWS account or by a wildcard principal. This configuration may provide persistent access if the principal can assume the role. The event alone does not establish that the role was assumed or that its creation was unauthorized.

Approved cross-account integrations and deliberately configured wildcard trust may match. Review the role owner, trust-policy conditions, role permissions, and whether the principal is expected.

eventSource="iam.amazonaws.com"
eventName="CreateRole"

| where isnull(errorCode) OR errorCode="" OR lower(errorCode)="success"

| eval policy_raw='requestParameters.assumeRolePolicyDocument',
       role_name='requestParameters.roleName',
       role_arn='responseElements.role.arn',
       user_arn=coalesce(user_arn, 'userIdentity.arn'),
       src=coalesce(src, sourceIPAddress)

| where isnotnull(policy_raw)

| eval policy=if(match(policy_raw, "(?i)^%7b"), urldecode(policy_raw), policy_raw)

| rex field=role_arn "^arn:[^:]+:iam::(?<account_from_role_arn>[0-9]{12}):role/"

| eval role_account_id=coalesce(account_from_role_arn, recipientAccountId)
| eval statements_json=json_extract(policy, "Statement")
| eval statements=if(match(statements_json, "^\s*\["), json_array_to_mv(statements_json), statements_json)

| mvexpand statements

| eval effect=json_extract(statements, "Effect"),
       actions_json=json_extract(statements, "Action"),
       principal_json=json_extract(statements, "Principal")

| eval actions=if(match(actions_json, "^\s*\["), json_array_to_mv(actions_json), actions_json)

| mvexpand actions
| mvexpand actions

| eval trust_action=actions
| eval action_pattern=replace(replace(lower(trust_action), "[*]", "%"), "[?]", "_")

| where effect="Allow" AND like("sts:assumerole", action_pattern)
| eval aws_json=json_extract(principal_json, "AWS")

| eval principals=case(
    match(aws_json, "^\s*\["), json_array_to_mv(aws_json),
    isnotnull(aws_json), aws_json,
    principal_json="*", "*"
  )

| mvexpand principals

| eval trust_principal=principals

| rex field=trust_principal "^arn:[^:]+:iam::(?<principal_account_from_arn>[0-9]{12}):"

| eval principal_account_id=if(
    match(trust_principal, "^[0-9]{12}$"),
    trust_principal,
    principal_account_from_arn
  )

| eval trust_type=case(
    trust_principal="*", "wildcard",
    isnotnull(role_account_id) AND isnotnull(principal_account_id)
      AND principal_account_id!=role_account_id, "external"
  )

| where trust_type="wildcard" OR trust_type="external"

| eval event_key=coalesce(eventID, md5(_raw))

| dedup event_key trust_principal

| table _time eventID user_arn src role_name role_arn role_account_id
        trust_type trust_principal principal_account_id trust_action

| `aws_iam_role_created_with_external_trust_filter`
