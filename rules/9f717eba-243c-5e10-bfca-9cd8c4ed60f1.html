AWS EKS Events Deleted

Last updated 5 days ago on 2026-10-20
Created 3 months ago on 2026-08-03

About

This rule detects the deletion of Kubernetes events, which can indicate an attempt to cover up malicious activity or misconfigurations. Adversaries may delete events to remove traces of their actions, making it harder for defenders to investigate and respond to incidents.

False Positives
Administrators may delete Kubernetes Event objects during routine troubleshooting or cleanup. Confirm the actor, scope, and timing before treating the activity as malicious.
