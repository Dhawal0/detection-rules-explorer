About

Identifies the creation of an Azure managed-disk snapshot ("Microsoft.Compute/snapshots/write"). Adversaries create snapshots of disks to stage data for exfiltration, copying or sharing the snapshot to an attacker-controlled scope without reading the source VM directly. This ES|QL rule normalizes the acting identity across user- and service-principal-initiated operations and excludes known cloud-provider egress networks.

esql code block:
✄𐘗FROM logs-azure.activitylogs-* METADATA _id, _version, _index
| WHERE event.dataset == "azure.activitylogs"
    AND event.action == "MICROSOFT.COMPUTE/SNAPSHOTS/WRITE"
    AND event.outcome == "success"
    AND NOT COALESCE(source.as.organization.name, "none") IN
        ("Google LLC", "Amazon.com, Inc.", "Microsoft Corporation", "GOOGLE-CLOUD-PLATFORM",
         "MICROSOFT-CORP-MSN-AS-BLOCK", "AMAZON-AES", "AMAZON-02")
| EVAL Esql.actor = COALESCE(user.name, azure.activitylogs.identity.authorization.evidence.principal_id)
| KEEP _id, _version, _index, @timestamp, Esql.actor, cloud.account.id, azure.resource.name, source.as.organization.name, event.action
