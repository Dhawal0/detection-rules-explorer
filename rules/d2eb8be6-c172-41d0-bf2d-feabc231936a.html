GitHub Payment Method Removed

Last updated 2 months ago on 2026-09-22
Created 2 months ago on 2026-09-22

About

Identifies the removal of a payment method from a GitHub organization or enterprise. Billing changes are high-privilege actions, and an adversary with billing-manager or owner access can delete a payment instrument to disrupt the organization's paid plan, which can lead to downgraded features, suspended private repositories, and loss of access to paid capabilities. The action may also be used to obscure fraudulent plan changes or to pressure the organization during an extortion or takeover scenario.
