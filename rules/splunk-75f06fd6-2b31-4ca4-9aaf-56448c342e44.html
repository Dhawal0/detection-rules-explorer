Cisco AI Defense Allowed Sensitive Prompt

Last updated 2 days ago on 2026-10-16

About

Detects Cisco AI Defense prompt events that were allowed and matched a privacy guardrail for personally identifiable information or payment card data. These events may indicate that sensitive information was submitted to an AI application and should be reviewed against the organization's data handling policy.

False Positives
Authorized testing, demonstrations, synthetic data, or approved workflows may submit content that matches PII or PCI guardrails. Review the application, connection, policy, guardrail entity, and data handling requirements before treating an event as a policy violation.

✄𐘗`cisco_ai_defense`
event_action="allow"
event_message_type="prompt"

| eval guardrail_ruleset_type=mvjoin('event_policy_guardrail_assocs{}.policy_guardrail_assoc.guardrail_avail_ruleset.guardrail_ruleset_type', ", "),

       guardrail_entity_name=mvjoin('event_policy_guardrail_assocs{}.policy_guardrail_assoc.guardrail_avail_entity.guardrail_entity_name', ", "),

       application_name=if(isnotnull('genai_application.application_name') AND len(trim('genai_application.application_name'))>0, 'genai_application.application_name', "unknown"),

       connection_name=if(isnotnull('connection.connection_name') AND len(trim('connection.connection_name'))>0, 'connection.connection_name', "unknown"),

       application_key=if(isnotnull(application_id) AND len(trim(application_id))>0, application_id, application_name),

       connection_key=if(isnotnull(connection_id) AND len(trim(connection_id))>0, connection_id, connection_name),

       policy_name='policy.policy_name',

       event_time=_time

| where mvfind('event_policy_guardrail_assocs{}.policy_guardrail_assoc.guardrail_avail_ruleset.guardrail_ruleset_type', "(?i)\\bPII\\b|personally identifiable information|\\bPCI\\b|payment card industry")>=0

| bin _time span=10m

| stats count as event_count
        dc(event_id) as distinct_event_count
        min(event_time) as firstTime
        max(event_time) as lastTime
        values(event_id) as event_ids
        values(guardrail_ruleset_type) as guardrail_ruleset_types
        values(guardrail_entity_name) as guardrail_entity_names
        values(policy_name) as policy_names
        values(application_name) as application_names
        values(connection_name) as connection_names
    by _time application_key connection_key

| `security_content_ctime(firstTime)`
| `security_content_ctime(lastTime)`

| table _time firstTime lastTime application_key application_names connection_key connection_names
        distinct_event_count event_count event_ids guardrail_ruleset_types guardrail_entity_names policy_names

| `cisco_ai_defense_allowed_sensitive_prompt_filter`
