GCP Vertex AI Role Granted to Principal

Last updated 16 days ago on 2026-10-23
Created 16 days ago on 2026-10-23

About

Identifies an IAM policy change on a Google Cloud project, folder, or organization that grants a Vertex AI role (`roles/aiplatform.*`) to a principal. Vertex AI roles control who can invoke models, deploy endpoints, run agents, and read prompt and training data. An adversary who has gained IAM administration rights can grant a Vertex AI role to an identity they control to keep model access after the original foothold is removed, or to run LLMjacking workloads under the victim's billing. Google-managed service agent roles are excluded because Google grants them automatically when the API is enabled.
