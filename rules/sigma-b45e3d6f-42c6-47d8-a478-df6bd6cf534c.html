Local System Accounts Discovery Attempt - Linux

Last updated 6 years ago on 2020-10-08
Created 6 years ago on 2020-10-08

About

Detects attempts to enumerate local user accounts via common Linux utilities and sensitive file access. Adversaries may read files such as /etc/passwd, /etc/shadow, and /etc/sudoers, or use tools like getent, id, who, and finger to identify accounts and privilege levels on a compromised system.
