Shell Evaluated Curl Downloaded Content

Last updated 13 days ago on 2026-11-10
Created 14 days ago on 2026-11-09

About

Detects a shell that downloads content with curl and passes it to eval, either directly or through a variable assignment. Downloading a script and evaluating it in memory avoids writing the payload to disk and is commonly used by macOS loaders, adware and infostealer stagers to retrieve and run their next stage.
